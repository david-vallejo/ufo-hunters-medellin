Privacy Policy

UFO Hunters: Medellín · Last updated October 15, 2026

The short version

The game doesn't ask for an account, and it doesn't collect, sell or share any personal information about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your best score, your selected ship and laser color, and how many rounds you've played are saved only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. The app only checks with the store whether you own the Full game, Ships pack or Lasers pack.

Services the game loads from

The game is delivered from GitHub Pages and loads its 3D engine from jsDelivr and its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when the game loads. Their own privacy policies apply.

Map data

The city is built from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and Mapzen/AWS terrain tiles. No location data is collected from you.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
